Ask: What evidence makes you so sure?

B · Evidence to verify

MUST, MIGHT OR CAN'T?

C · Past deduction

MODAL + HAVE + P.P.

Explain one decision filmmakers made in the past.

Check: has the time changed the verb form?

Fact: Canada offers tax credits to film producers.
Inference: Canada might be a cheaper location because tax credits reduce costs.

Step 4 · Four-person committee · 8 min

Build the recommendation.

Two pairs merge. Each member checks one part before the team is cleared to pitch.

08:00

Evidence keeper

Verify every detail against the listening notes.

Language checker

Check the modal and tense against certainty and time.

Logic checker

Check that each reason leads to the stated result.

Speaker

Deliver the final argument clearly within 60 seconds.

CCLAIM

We recommend filming in ______.

EEVIDENCE

RREASON

Since ______, ______. Therefore, ______.

±CERTAINTY + LIMIT

This location must / might be suitable because ______. However, ______ might still be a concern.

We recommend filming in Canada. The presentation states that filmmakers can receive tax credits there. Since these credits reduce production costs, Canada is an attractive location. Therefore, the company may save money by filming there. Canada must be a strong option, although we still need information about labour and environmental costs.
Say“Do not vote yet. First make every link in your argument auditable.”

Step 5 · Hearing and vote · 10 min

Test the argument. Then decide.

Pitch for 60 seconds. Another team draws one question. The jury checks evidence, language and logic.

01:00

Draw one challenge question after each pitch.

Fact-checker / peer jury

  • Country, cause and result are accurate.
  • The claim is clear.
  • Listening evidence supports the claim.
  • Modal and tense match the certainty.
  • Cause and effect are logically connected.

Say“Challenge the evidence, not the speaker. A question should help the claim become more precise.”

Take-away · 2 min

Good speaking begins with accurate listening.

五个知识点不是五道孤立的题，而是一条从输入到决策的完整思维链。
